Tripeer.

A modern community directory and local gathering platform designed around intentional connection, peer-vetted trust, and thoughtful exploration.

tripeer.com — Interactive Prototype
Interactive Sandbox.

Destigmatizing Community Discovery

Digital platforms in the psychedelic space often fall into two extremes: sterile clinical portals or loud, neon counterculture clichés. Both create friction for everyday explorers looking for local community, shared experiences, and trusted companions.

Tripper replaces visual noise with a clean, welcoming interface built around real human connection—making it easy to find your tribe, share experiences, and build genuine local community.
